# Crunchify visual direction, revision 2

These early concepts were superseded by the [Drive-based visual direction](VISUAL-DIRECTION-V3.md).

These are static, private visual concepts, not a functioning or approved website.

- Use a warm off-white canvas, clear grid, generous spacing, thin dividers, and restrained rounded corners. Let photography and the wordmark provide the personality.
- Keep the brand book's brown `#8F5035` and cream `#FFF0C7` prominent. The darker approved broccoli palette color `#566C30` is used for small green text and green buttons; white on that color has an approximate 5.86:1 contrast ratio. White on the kit's `#1C925D` is approximately 3.95:1, so avoid it for small normal-size button text without an accessible treatment. Check actual component colors and all states during implementation.
- The supplied kit specifies Talina Regular and Antipasto Pro; the provided files are labeled demo/trial. Confirm web and commercial licensing before using them in production. The concepts use Talina selectively and Poppins (also supplied) for legibility; confirm this treatment with the company.
- The draft included a separate banana and coconut illustration. Later revisions used the supplied studio photography.
- The shared kit includes white-pouch mockups and a different yellow Banana package. Verify which is current before final implementation. Apple pack size also requires confirmation.
- Maintain the complete inquiry fields and truthful error/success states. Do not claim an unimplemented site stores inquiries securely or that the CRM, email, Vercel preview or VPS work is complete.

The `wireframes-v2/` folder holds fourteen redesigned boards plus `15-ingredient-illustration.png`. The prior thirteen boards remain in `wireframes/`. The shared Drive folder's original thirteen file IDs were updated with revision 2 and the two additional images were added.
